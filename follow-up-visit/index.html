Follow-up visits in Oregon, WI

Shorter, focused appointments that keep your care moving in the right direction.

What happens at a follow-up visit?

A quick re-check with instrumentation and palpation, then a specific adjustment to whatever the re-check identifies. Much shorter than the first visit.

Follow-up visits are short and specific. We re-check the segments we are working on, adjust what needs adjusting, and leave the rest alone.

  1. A quick re-check

    Instrumentation and palpation again, so each adjustment is aimed at what your spine is doing today rather than what it did last month.

  2. The adjustment

    Specific and by hand, to the segments the re-check identifies.

  3. Progress, out loud

    What has changed since last time, and whether the plan should change with it.

Why consistency does the work

Spinal problems rarely resolve on their own, and how quickly yours responds depends on how long it has been there, what you do all day, and how your body heals. Coming consistently early on is what makes the difference; the visits typically space out as things hold.

  • Book ahead to get the times that suit you.
  • Tell us about changes between visits, good or bad.
  • Keep going through the stretch where you feel better but are not finished.

How care works

  1. We look first

    The analysis comes before any adjustment, every time.

  2. We explain what we found

    In plain language, with the imaging in front of you.

  3. We agree a plan

    A recommendation with a frequency, which you decide on.

Common questions

How many visits will I need?

It varies genuinely, which is why we will not quote a number before examining you. Your plan is set at the second visit and reviewed as you go.

How long does a follow-up take?

Much shorter than the first visit. Most people are in and out quickly.
