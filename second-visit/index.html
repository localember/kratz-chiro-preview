Your Second Visit: Report of Findings

About thirty minutes: what we found, what it means, and what we recommend.

Call (608) 291-1037 Request an appointment

What is the second visit for?

It is the report of findings. We explain your analysis, answer your questions, and recommend a care plan with a clear frequency.

The second visit is where the findings get explained in plain language. You should leave it understanding your own spine well enough to describe it to someone else.

  1. Your findings, explained

    We go through the analysis with you, showing what we saw and why it matters.

  2. Your questions

    Anything you have thought of since the first visit. This is the appointment for it.

  3. Your goals

    Relief from a specific problem, or staying well over the longer term. The plan differs, so we ask rather than assume.

  4. A care plan with a frequency

    How often, for roughly how long, and what we expect to see change. Written down, not vague.

Talking about cost

We would rather have the money conversation openly at the start than have it become a reason to stop care that is working. Payment options are discussed at this visit. We have plans available for all kinds of insurance.

Ask what a full course of care is likely to cost before you begin. Any practice worth attending will answer plainly.

What you can expect here

Explained as we go

You are told what we are doing and why, at each step, rather than after.

Specific, not general

The Gonstead method adjusts the segment the analysis identifies and leaves the rest alone.

How care works

  1. We look first

    The analysis comes before any adjustment, every time.

  2. We explain what we found

    In plain language, with your findings in front of you.

  3. We agree a plan

    A recommendation with a frequency, which you decide on.

Common questions

How long is the second visit?

Around thirty minutes. It is a conversation rather than an examination.

Do I have to commit to a plan on the day?

No. The plan is a recommendation. You decide what you want to do with it, and you can change your mind later.
